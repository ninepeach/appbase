# Diagnostics and wake ingress
App owns a bounded logger and explicit wake router. Logs are event codes, not arbitrary payloads; callers must not encode secrets in codes. Crash capture is optional and process-wide; it cannot guarantee capture during OOM, native crashes, deadlocks or OS termination. Wake routing is serialized with immediate BUSY on contention; successful IDs are retained until expiry within a bounded capacity. External adapters must authenticate and validate payloads before dispatch. No durable delivery guarantee. Lifecycle events are best effort; consumers reconcile from current phase. UI is not initialized by the core wake router. Android/iOS adapters and lifecycle policies remain future work.
